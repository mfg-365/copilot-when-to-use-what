The organizing idea

One spectrum: simpler & faster → more autonomous

Every Copilot surface trades speed and control for autonomy and scope. The further right you go, the more the system does on its own across more steps, sources and time.

← Simpler and fasterMore autonomous →
Copilot ChatAsk, find, draft one thing
CoworkMulti-artifact, multi-step
CodeBuild software
Microsoft ScoutAlways-on, runs your day
Custom AgentsReusable, governed capability

Rule of thumb

Reach for the leftmost surface that can do the job. Move right only when the task needs more autonomy, more artifacts, more systems, or a longer time horizon than the tool to its left can handle.

The agentic tier

Cowork, Code & autopilots

As you move right on the spectrum, Copilot shifts from answering to acting. These three share Work IQ grounding and multi-model reasoning, but differ in what they produce and how autonomously they run.

Cowork
  • Grounded in Work IQ
  • Multi-model
  • Microsoft 365 apps integration
  • Image generation
  • Third-party plugins
  • Sensitivity labels, Communication Compliance, DSPM, audit logging, eDiscovery

Best for: multi-artifact creation in one task.

Code
  • Natural-language software building grounded in Work IQ
  • Local or cloud development
  • Multi-model
  • Plugins and connectors

Best for: multi-step business process expressed as software.

Autopilots · Scout
  • Cloud-hosted agent with its own identity + local desktop companion app
  • Works 24/7, runs habits and automations
  • Powered by Work IQ, with access to M365 productivity apps
  • “Multi-player” — others can interact with your Scout (book time, share updates)

Best for: always-on manage & coordinate my day.

Where custom agents fit

If you find yourself running the same Cowork or Scout task repeatedly — especially one others on your team need too — that's the signal to consider a custom agent in Agent Builder or Copilot Studio, where the workflow, knowledge and permissions become a governed, shareable capability.
