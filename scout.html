Surface · Always-on autonomy

Microsoft Scout

A cloud-hosted agent with its own identity and a local desktop companion app. Scout works 24/7 — running your habits and automations, powered by Work IQ with access to your Microsoft 365 productivity apps — and coordinates your day even while you're away.

When to choose Scout

✅ Reach for Scout when…

  • You want work to happen always-on, not just when you're in a chat
  • You want a persistent agent with its own identity running habits & automations
  • Tasks should coordinate your day across time and systems
  • You want “multi-player” scenarios — others interact with your Scout (book time, share updates)
  • You need a local desktop companion alongside the cloud agent

⛔ Use something else when…

  • It's a one-off task you're doing right now → Chat
  • You need multiple artifacts in a single session → Cowork
  • A simple recurring prompt is enough → Cowork scheduled prompt
  • You're building software → Code

⏳

👥

Multi-player

Other people can interact with your Scout — e.g. book time or share updates.

Scout vs. Cowork

Cowork is a powerful in-session operator you drive and approve. Scout is persistent and autonomous — the right move when the value is in it running on its own over time, coordinating your day, and being reachable by others.

Scout vs. a custom agent

Scout is your always-on operator. When an autonomous workflow needs to be standardized, shared and governed for a whole team — with fixed knowledge, actions and permissions — that's the signal to consider a custom agent instead.
