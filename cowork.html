Copilot Cowork

Describe a task in natural language and Cowork does the work across Microsoft 365 — drafting emails, scheduling meetings, creating documents, posting in Teams and managing files. It creates multiple artifacts in one task and takes actions only after you approve them.

Light · Medium · Heavy Watch it work

✅ Reach for Cowork when…

  • One request should yield several artifacts (e.g. a brief + an Excel + a deck)
  • It's a multi-step process across systems — email, calendar, files, Teams
  • You want it to take actions (send, post, schedule) with your approval
  • You want a recurring task to run on a schedule or on an event trigger
  • You need deep research compiled into finished deliverables

⛔ Use something else when…

  • You just need a quick answer or one draft → Chat
  • It should run always-on and coordinate your day → Scout
  • You're building software → Code
  • The same workflow is reused org-wide → Custom agent

Cowork works in the cloud, with your approval

Cowork acts across OneDrive, SharePoint, Outlook and Teams — not your local device — and asks before any sensitive action (send, post, schedule). Always review the approval dialog: recipients, content, timing.

Cowork task types

From quick recurring wins to deep, multi-output analysis

Tasks scale by number of sources, time frame, and deliverables — from a quick recurring win to a deep, multi-output analysis.

Light tasks

A handful of knowledge sources and light reasoning — like a summary — usually one short deliverable.

  • Few sources
  • Light reasoning
  • 1 or fewer outputs
“Create a weekly status update for my team that runs every Monday — including my top-of-mind priorities and a short list of key executive meetings pulled from my calendar. Save as a draft.”

Medium tasks

Many sources over varying timeframes, structured reasoning, and multiple deliverables.

  • Many sources
  • Structured reasoning
  • 2+ outputs
“Help me prepare for a customer meeting by pulling relevant emails, calendar items and recent files into a briefing doc based on our template, an Excel overview of their sales and trends, and a client-ready presentation.”

Heavy tasks

Deep analysis and broad source aggregation over long time frames, generating many outputs.

  • Broad aggregation
  • Deep reasoning
  • Many outputs
“Analyze 6 months of exported product usage data to understand usage patterns, classify prompts, and produce a leadership-ready report including a comparison to the prior six-month period.”

What Cowork does

Acts across Microsoft 365 — via skills

Cowork loads specialized skills as it works (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, PDF, Email, Calendar, Meetings, Daily Briefing, Enterprise Search, Deep Research, and more). You can add your own custom skills and extend it with plugins from the Microsoft 365 App Store.

✉

Communication

Draft & send email, post in Teams channels/chats, send HTML newsletters, manage your inbox.

📄

Documents & files

Create and edit Word, Excel, PowerPoint and PDFs; browse Work IQ; organize SharePoint/OneDrive.

📅

Calendar & meetings

Schedule and manage your calendar, resolve conflicts, prep meeting intelligence and daily briefings.

🔎

Research & search

Search across your org and run deep research that synthesizes many sources into comprehensive reports.

⏳

Automation

Run prompts on a schedule (“every morning at 9 AM”) or on event triggers (a new email or Teams message).

🧩

Extend

Create up to 50 custom skills stored in OneDrive; install plugins for specialized expertise and connectors.

Cowork scheduled prompts vs. Microsoft Scout

Cowork can run recurring prompts, which covers many “do this every week” needs. Choose Microsoft Scout instead when you want a persistent agent with its own identity that runs habits and automations continuously, coordinates your day, and lets others interact with it.
